Receipt Vault — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026 · App: Receipt Vault (com.chiramdasu.receiptvault)

Receipt Vault is designed so that your information never leaves your phone. This policy explains, in plain terms, what the app does and does not do with your data.

Data stored only on your device

Receipts you scan or enter — including photos, store names, dates, amounts, items, and warranty dates — are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. They are not uploaded to any server, and the developer cannot see them.

On-device text recognition (OCR)

Receipt scanning uses text-recognition software that runs entirely on your device. Receipt images are processed locally and are never transmitted for scanning or analysis.

No accounts, no tracking

The app has no user accounts and no sign-in. It contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Camera permission

The app requests camera access only so you can photograph receipts for scanning. Photos are used solely for the scan you initiate and are stored only as described above. You may instead pick an existing image from your device.

Data we collect

None. Because everything is stored locally and nothing is transmitted, the developer collects no personal data through this app. If you contact us by email, we will see the information you choose to include in that message.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual receipts inside the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all of its locally stored data from your device.

Children

The app is a general-purpose household utility and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
